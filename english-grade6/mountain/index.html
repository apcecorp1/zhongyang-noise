🏔️ A Trip to the Mountains

📖 英文閱讀

Last Sunday, my family went hiking in the mountains near our city. We woke up at six o'clock because we wanted to start before the weather became hot. My mother packed sandwiches, bananas, and bottles of water. My father checked the map and the weather forecast. I put on my hiking shoes and carried a small backpack. My younger sister brought a camera to take pictures of plants and animals.

When we arrived at the trail, the air was cool and fresh. We could hear birds singing in the trees. At first, the path was wide and easy to walk on. After thirty minutes, it became narrow and steep. I was tired, so I asked my father if we could take a short break. He smiled and said, "Of course. Hiking is not a race." We sat on a rock, drank some water, and enjoyed the beautiful forest.

Later, we saw a squirrel running across the path. My sister wanted to follow it, but my mother reminded us to stay on the trail. She explained that wild animals need space and that people should not feed them. We also found a few plastic bottles on the ground. We picked them up and put them in a trash bag. Keeping the mountains clean is everyone's responsibility.

After two hours, we finally reached the top. The view was amazing! We could see green hills, small houses, and a river far below. The wind was strong, so we put on our jackets. We ate our lunch and took a family photo. Before going down, we checked that we had all our things. We walked slowly because the rocks were slippery.

That evening, my legs felt sore, but I was proud of myself. I learned that preparation, teamwork, and patience are important when hiking. I also learned to respect nature. I hope we can explore another mountain together next month.

🇹🇼 中文翻譯（最後才看）

上星期日，我和家人到城市附近的山區健行。我們早上六點起床，想趁天氣還沒變熱前出發。媽媽準備了三明治、香蕉和水。爸爸查看地圖和天氣預報。我穿上登山鞋，背著小背包。妹妹帶著相機拍攝動植物。

到達步道時，空氣涼爽又清新。我們聽見鳥兒唱歌。一開始道路寬敞好走，三十分鐘後變得狹窄又陡峭。我累了，問爸爸能否休息。他說：「當然可以。健行不是比賽。」我們坐在石頭上喝水，欣賞森林。

後來，我們看見松鼠跑過步道。妹妹想追過去，媽媽提醒我們留在步道上。野生動物需要空間，人們不該餵食牠們。我們撿起地上的塑膠瓶，放進垃圾袋。保持山林清潔是每個人的責任。

兩小時後，我們終於抵達山頂。景色令人驚嘆！我們看見綠色山丘、小房子和遠方的河流。風很強，我們穿上外套，吃午餐、拍全家福。下山前確認帶齊物品，因為岩石濕滑，我們慢慢走。

晚上我的腿很痠，但我很自豪。我學到準備、團隊合作和耐心的重要，也學會尊重大自然。希望下個月還能和家人一起探索另一座山。
